## 观察与测试日志摘要

### 1. 离线二进制移植执行
```text
$ python3 re_kernel_x/tools/patch_offsets.py baseline \
    artifacts/re_kernel_x-1.6+ge112656f7639.r72953914.kpb51197a.ndk26.3.11579264/re_kernel_x_1.6.kpm \
    --output local/ported-5.15/re_kernel_x_1.6.kpm.json
{"binder_abi": 6, "table_bytes": 90, "sha256": "79e81dccca796c83257191b965e7cc493892b8c213bbc28719719f6c2c874663"}

$ python3 re_kernel_x/tools/patch_offsets.py patch \
    artifacts/re_kernel_x-1.6+ge112656f7639.r72953914.kpb51197a.ndk26.3.11579264/re_kernel_x_1.6.kpm \
    --layout local/ported-5.15/re_kernel_x_1.6.kpm.json \
    --offsets local/ported-5.15/target-offsets-5.15.json \
    --output local/ported-5.15/re_kernel_x_1.6_ported_5.15.kpm
{"binder_abi": 5, "table_bytes": 90, "sha256": "fa31ae3b1da403c1b01071da5e7b2918084b18464a60f15d50ee91dd5860331c"}
```
